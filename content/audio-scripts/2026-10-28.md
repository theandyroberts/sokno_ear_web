## Script (TTS-ready)

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, and Severe Avenue is treating the whole weekend like the holiday. Andy Roberts here with your South Knoxville Events and Rumors.

The big one is Saturday: the Old Severe District's Halloween Pub Crawl, eleven neighborhood stops on one passport, all day long. Pick up a passport at any participating spot, get it stamped when you buy something, and visit six or more to be entered for a prize package from the district's businesses. It's free to play and costumes are highly encouraged. The stops are Alliance Brewing, Fly by Night, Hi-Wire, Honeybee Coffee, Printshop, Redbud Kitchen, Severe Avenue Burger Company, South Coast Pizza, SouthSide Garage, the Pink Cactus and Trailhead Beer Market, so there's coffee and pizza on the route, not just beer. Park once, at the Riverwalk garage or the gravel lot under the bridge, and walk it. It's mostly cloudy and seventy-six, with a chance of showers after dark.

Thursday gets things started at Puckers, the sports grill on Mimosa Avenue. Boos and Brews runs five thirty to nine thirty: a costume contest with a cash prize, pumpkin beer pong, shot roulette and a live D J. Pucker Hour runs five to six, with a dollar Puckers Lager and three-dollar wells.

Fly by Night, the seventies cocktail lounge on Severe Avenue, has two nights. Thursday, Wax Museum, that's Xerox the Kidd and Country Dark, spin spooky vinyl rarities, no cover posted, and the creepy cocktail list runs all month. Friday at nine it's the lounge's fourth annual Freaks Come Out at Night ball, with Alex Falk, Minitiger and Benicio on the decks. Ten dollars, twenty-one and up, dress up and get down.

The 808, the new music venue at 808 Severe Avenue, has a free night and a ticketed one. Friday is a free nineties rave with Psychonaut, doors at seven, eighteen and up, no ticket. Saturday, Halloween night, it's Bear Grillz, the dubstep headliner who performs as a bear, with Spnce and Shosstah opening, doors at seven, eighteen and up. Tickets are on the venue's site.

The Pink Cactus has something all three nights. Friday at seven thirty, Knox Social Club and the Comedy Sphere host Love at First Laugh, a blind-dating gameshow run by comedians, and at nine Planet Doom takes over with Pink Is Punk, a HalloweEMO party of E D M remixes of emo favorites until two. Eighteen and up, costumes encouraged; a thirty-five dollar V I P ticket covers both. Saturday it's Belltower's haunted house party: goth D Js downstairs, free karaoke in all four rooms after nine, vendors, the outdoor bar, fifteen dollars at the gate, eighteen and up, and they're partying until the clock says two, with the extra hour from the time change. And Sunday from five to nine, the pink house celebrates Día de los Muertos with a free cumbia class, free catrina face paint and a traditional ofrenda. Bring the family for that one.

Hi-Wire Brewing hosts Drop Dead, a Halloween drag show and costume contest, Saturday from nine to eleven thirty in the taproom on Barber Street. Tickets are about ten dollars on the brewery's ticket page.

Trailhead Beer Market on Island Home Avenue has music outdoors all three nights, free: the Jake Smith Trio Friday at seven, Four Legged Dog on Halloween night at seven, and the Mount Olive Pleasure Club closes the weekend Sunday at four. Gato Peligroso, the kitchen inside, has its mushroom menu with Extraterrestrial Fungi every night through Sunday.

Friday night at Kerns, the lawn goes country for the Ho'Down: line dancing, two-stepping, a live D J, a mechanical bull and a group lesson in the Thriller line dance. The costume contest winners are announced at nine, and first prize is a five-hundred-dollar gift card.

Four things at Imes Nature Center. Friday from seven to nine is the Starlight Paddle, two guided hours on the river after dark with the kayak, the life vest and the headlamp provided, forty-five dollars, ages fourteen and up, meeting at the River Landing dock. Saturday morning is for the kids: a free bat storytime at the Nature Playscape from ten to noon, and at eleven, Playing Possum, an hour with the park's newest animal ambassador, an opossum, ten dollars. And Sunday from ten to noon, Renew in Nature: yoga, a sound bath and a forest bathing walk at the homesite, forty dollars. Showers are likely Sunday; the yoga and the sound bath are under roof.

Earl's on Waterfront Drive runs a different deal every day: ten-dollar puh-trone mango ritas Thursday, nine-dollar spicy pineapple ritas Friday, eight-dollar house margaritas on Halloween, and seven-dollar mimosas and Bloody Marys Sunday morning. No game this Saturday; the Vols are off until Kentucky comes to town.

That's the weekend. Set the clocks back Saturday night, keep the costume on an extra hour, and I'll see you around Sohno!
