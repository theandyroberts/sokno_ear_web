## Script (TTS-ready)

Bring your applause, So-No — Andy Roberts here with your South Knoxville Ear, and this weekend the neighborhood says a proper goodbye to a summer tradition.

The thirty-sixth Knoxville Shakespeare festival plays its final four nights on the lawn at Eye-ams Park. King Henry V goes Thursday — and Thursday is pay-what-you-can, so the cheapest seat in town is the first one — then The Merry Wives of Windsor Friday, the last-ever Henry V on Saturday, and Sunday night, Merry Wives takes the festival's final bow. Doors at seven, curtain seven-thirty, kids under fifteen free, and bring a chair — the lawn is the seating. After Sunday, the lawn goes back to the fireflies.

One piece of business before the theater: Thursday is Election Day. State and federal primaries, polls open eight in the morning to eight at night. They close half an hour after curtain, so the truly efficient can vote on the way to Agincourt.

Over at Kerns, the boots are back — the Ho'down Social returns Friday night at eight, and it's now a standing date, every other Friday into December. Before the dancing, The Big Pink plays the music of The Band starting at five. Jazz takes Saturday, easy covers take Sunday afternoon.

Eye-ams Park has a gentler side this weekend too. Saturday brings free storytelling for the kids at ten. And Sunday might be the softest morning of the year: a sound bath at ten, a stained-glass workshop at eleven, and yoga in the trees at eleven-thirty. Fair warning — Friday's evening paddle is already sold out, so when the next one posts, don't dawdle.

The specials board: Earl's runs a different deal every day — ten-dollar Patrón ritas Thursday, nine-dollar spicy ritas Friday, eight-dollar margs all day Saturday, and seven-dollar mimosas and Bloody Marys all day Sunday. And Sunday at two, Alliance Brewing hosts Bow Wow Bingo — beer and bingo, for the dogs.

Circle next Saturday too: the Hummingbird Festival lands at Eye-ams Park on August fifteenth, with live bird banding from seven to one.

That's the weekend. Keep your ears open — and I'll see you around So-No!
