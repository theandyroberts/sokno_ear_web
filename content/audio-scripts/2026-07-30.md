## Script (TTS-ready)

This one's for everybody with mud on their tires. Andy Roberts here with your South Knoxville Ear — and this week, the Ear finally goes into the woods.

The Urban Wilderness — sixty miles of trail in the neighborhood's back yard — gets the front page, with a trail report checked the morning we published. The short version: Baker Creek Bike Park is riding well, Marie Myers is open with one small detour, and Baker Creek Preserve needs a dry day — riding muddy trail is how good berms die. Next, a correction, and it's the happy kind. Last week we said Shakespeare was closing at Eye-ams Park. We were wrong — the run goes through August ninth. That means all four nights this weekend, Thursday is still pay-what-you-can, kids under fifteen free. Our mistake; your gain.

Some neighborhood news: that variance sign on Jonathan Avenue? Approved, four to nothing, nobody opposed. And the two-hundred-twenty-unit Kerbela apartment plan finally has its committee date — August thirteenth. We'll be watching.

Friday, Kerns throws a Back to School Bash from eleven to three — inflatables, face painting, and a school-supply drive, so bring a pack of pencils. Then it's jazz all weekend: the Gruuv Series Friday night, the Sky Hatcher Trio Saturday, Jason Ellis Sunday afternoon.

At Eye-ams Park, Saturday is a whole curriculum — tree identification at nine-thirty, storytelling at ten, EcoArt for the kids at three — and Sunday morning the bird banders are back at seven-thirty, with yoga in the trees at eleven-thirty.

And the specials board is loaded: margaritas at Earl's, four-dollar drafts at Hi-Wire Thursday, music bingo at So-No Taco, and bottomless brunch at Mimosas both mornings.

That's the week. Keep your ears open — and I'll see you around So-No!
