## Script (TTS-ready)

Four sunny days in a row, a home game on Saturday, and the neighborhood has something on every night. Andy Roberts here with your South Knoxville Events and Rumors.

Start with the free one that gets talked about. Friday at seven, Hi-Wire on Barber Street brings back Pitch a Friend. You sign up a single friend, build a few slides, and stand up in the taproom to make the case: who they are, what they want, and why somebody in the room should ask them out. Pitch spots are limited and presenters drink free. If you'd rather watch, come watch, and stick around for the singles mixer after. Free to attend.

The 808, the music venue at the top of Severe Avenue, has three touring nights in a row. Thursday it's lespecial, the Connecticut trio that calls itself progtronic, with Natalie Brooke opening, doors at seven, eighteen and up. Friday it's Deadtronica, which is Aron Magner of The Disco Biscuits and the guitarist Cloudchord playing Grateful Dead songs as dance music, doors at seven, eighteen and up. And Saturday it's a homecoming for Summoner's Circle, the symphonic metal band, with War Clown, NinjaWitch and Truckstop Dickpill, four Knoxville bands, all ages, doors at seven, well after the Vols finish with L S U. Tickets for all three on the venue's page, and our parking map is on the site.

Fly by Night, the seventies cocktail lounge on Severe Avenue, has two. Thursday at nine it's the Super Birthday gran prix, four birthdays in one week: the owner, Jocelyn, and the D Js Benicio, Minitiger and Tiny. Five dollars at the door. Saturday at nine, two live electronic acts from Athens, Georgia, Marcel Sletten and Organically Programmed, on their first tour together, the night after Asheville.

The Pink Cactus, the karaoke-room bar with the big front yard, has three nights with Planet Doom and friends. Friday at nine it's yee E D M, which the bar calls a country E D M crossover and the promoter calls Doomtown. Saturday at eight, First Bass times Planet Doom comes back after filling the place in October: two stages, one inside and a new one in the yard, ten D Js, and a guest from Las Vegas, Nofvcks Bivens. Tickets for both on Doomsayer's site. And Sunday from nine to midnight, Goos three plays house music decade by decade in his first set at the pink house, right after trivia at six thirty.

Friday from five to eight at Printshop on Island Home Avenue, the pizza truck Piethagorean cooks a benefit dinner on site: four pizzas off the stone and a Caesar salad bar, all you can eat, twenty-three dollars, and every dollar of profit goes to Ujima Soccer Academy, the East Knoxville youth program. Friday is also twelve-dollar flight day at the brewery.

Saturday morning, Sohno Sourdough opens on a Saturday for the first time. Nine to one, bagels and bread, up the new stairs on the loading dock between Redbud and Hi-Wire on Barber Street, and the bakery says it's every Saturday from here on.

Sunday at three, Visualize Art Studio brings a stained glass workshop to Hi-Wire. Two hours, a pre-cut kit, you pick a pumpkin, a cowboy boot or a bat, learn to foil and solder, and take it home. Eighty dollars, fourteen and up, book through the studio.

Over at Kerns, Thursday at six thirty is Twilight on the big screen, the second half of a free double feature. Narnia plays Wednesday. Grab a plate from the hall, and if the lot is full, Kerns says the Riverwalk Garage on Kay Street is a three-minute walk and free after six.

Four things at Imes Nature Center, all small groups, so register on the park's page. Saturday at one, Playing Possum: the park introduces its newest animal ambassador, an opossum, then everybody draws one. Ten dollars, ages five and up. Saturday at two thirty, Painting with the Sun teaches cyanotype, the old blueprint process. You forage your own leaves, lay them on the paper, expose it in the sun and keep the prints. Eighteen dollars, fourteen and up. Sunday from ten to noon at the Homesite, Renew in Nature is a yoga flow, then a sound bath, then a slow forest-bathing walk, forty dollars, bring a mat and a layer, it's forty-one degrees at ten. And Sunday at one, a naturalist leads a Moss Meander around Mead's Quarry, two hours learning the mosses on the rocks, fifteen dollars, sixteen and up. Sunny and sixty-seven for that one.

Earl's on Waterfront Drive runs a different deal every day, and Saturday the game-day specials come out for L S U at Neyland, kickoff at three thirty: two dollars off drafts, six-dollar Orange Bliss cocktails, six beer buckets for the price of five, two dollars off pizza, and eight-dollar house margaritas on top. Thursday is ten-dollar puh-trone mango ritas, Friday nine-dollar spicy pineapple ritas, and Sunday seven-dollar mimosas and Bloody Marys. Up on Severe, Printshop takes a dollar off pints from kickoff to the final whistle, and Kerns has the game on the big screens.

That's the weekend. Pitch the friend, meet the opossum, bring a jacket for the yard stage, and I'll see you around Sohno!
