Why 43 pages are left out on purpose
Each episode’s lead story also has its own page, with the same text as the episode page. Those pages tell Google to credit the episode page instead, so Google indexes one copy, not two. Google lists them as “alternate page with proper canonical”. That is the correct result, not a problem.

What the robots are doing
OpenAI's search crawler makes the most requests by far (6,205, because it loads every image and script). It re-reads the homepage about 25 times a day, which is how ChatGPT knows what's on this week. Amazon reads the most different pages. 38 times, ChatGPT opened a page live because someone asked it a question, most often about the Dirty South party and the Pink Cactus shows. Claude read 119 pages in one sweep on Nov 5. Perplexity barely visits.
How sure this is
A bot can lie about its name, so requests that were probing for passwords or config files were thrown out. The server keeps only 15 days of logs and shares one log with other sites, so the Ear's traffic was picked out by its page addresses. OpenAI's crawler runs the page code, so some Umami page views are robots, not readers. Google doesn't yet separate AI Overview clicks for this site.
