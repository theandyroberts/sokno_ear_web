Why 43 pages are left out on purpose
Each episode’s lead story also has its own page, with the same text as the episode page. Those pages tell Google to credit the episode page instead, so Google indexes one copy, not two. Google lists them as “alternate page with proper canonical”. That is the correct result, not a problem.
What would move these numbers
46 pages are found but not yet crawled, mostly July stories. Google gets to them with time and inbound links. Most searches that find the Ear are for events: Scruffy Roots Festival (21 appearances in 28 days), Spooky Streets (12), Borderland Playhouse (15). Timely event stories are what pull new readers in.

1Checks the site itself

  • Sitemap serves and grows with each episode. Every URL in it returns 200
  • robots.txt only hides /next, /draft/, /desk/
  • Canonicals: the newest episode credits the homepage. Older episodes and stories credit themselves. Feature stories credit their episode
  • Every story carries NewsArticle data, so Google reads it as news
  • Nothing public is accidentally hidden. www and http forward to soknoear.com

2Reads Google Search Console

  • Opens the property as the note15 Google account. The sparkpoint account has no access
  • Page indexing: how many pages are in, why the rest aren't, any new reason
  • Performance: clicks, appearances, top searches and pages

!When it alerts you

  • Anything on the site is broken
  • Anything it should read can't be reached: Search Console, the server, the site, a signed-out browser
  • Something needs your decision or your login
  • Every run until it's fixed. Email plus a phone push, one line: what's wrong, how old, the one fix

When everything works it sends nothing, so an alert always means something.
