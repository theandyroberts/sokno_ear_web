## 3. What changed

### Slot strings follow the clock change *(shipped this run, from the No. 22 staging on Wed Nov 11)*
Finding 2. `scripts/ig-schedule.mjs`: `etParts(ms)`, `etIso(day, hour, minute)` and `toIso(ms)` build every slot string with the `America/New_York` offset the calendar gives for that date, from `Intl`, independent of the machine's zone (the VPS runs UTC and the queue sets `process.env.TZ`; neither matters now). `scripts/ig-queue.mjs` uses them in the eight places it wrote a slot by hand (`iso`, `dateForDay`, the undated anchor, the venue carousel's day, the timing arms, the spacing pass, the lead window, the teaser). A rebuild of the No. 21 queue on this machine writes `-05:00` on every post.
