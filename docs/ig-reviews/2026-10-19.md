## 2. Findings

**1. Two weeks, eight shared posts, and they are the account.** 35 posts since Oct 7; the eight the venue passed on average 153, the twenty tagged-but-not-shared average 19, the three with nobody to tag 13. This week it was Kern's (a regular, twelve posts at 13 before this one, who shared the free family feature to five shares) and Printshop (two for two). Fly by Night, the Pink Cactus and Alliance, who carried last week, did not share this week's posts; the Pink Cactus shared the *story link* on Facebook instead, which sent 40 readers to the site and nothing to the Instagram post. The venue's choice is still the variable the pipeline cannot control; what it controls is who is tagged and whether the venue is told.

**2. Followers +39 without buying them.** Outbound follows +12, followers +39, ratio 1.10 lifetime for the first time. Thursday and Friday's 18 new followers arrived after the big Wednesday on profile visits (46 in the window). Followers who saw a post: 24%. The account's reach is non-followers (95% over 28 days) and the follower count is the residue.

**3. The Reel was built right and cut wrong.** Container path, publish, cover, caption from `reel.json`: all as designed, first time. The cut was 116 seconds and the audience watched 12. A Reel's distribution is a function of completion, so a two-minute list of eighteen things is the one format Instagram will not carry. Andy liked the format ("I like the format … say go to SoKnoEAR.com"); the fix is length, not format. 45 seconds holds the feature and one story a day, the trimmed intro says "five of the eighteen things … all of them at soknoear.com", and the outro says it again, which is his "middle and end".

**4. The artist-tag change was never fed.** The Oct 12 review shipped `userTagsFor(tags, { everyone })` and wrote step 1h into the Tuesday task. The task ran Oct 13 and added no act handle to any story or to the registry, so Wednesday tagged venues alone and the measure ("every No. 18 story with a named act carries its handle") reads 0 of 9. A rule in a task file is a suggestion; the research check is a gate, and the Ijams/Old Sevier rules only started working when they became one. So `social.igAct` is now required and checked (section 3).

**5. The roundup is working now.** 14 → 32 → 68, five profile visits, a follow. The caption tags nine venues; it is the one post every venue gets a notification about. It stays.

**6. Saturday is where the drip dies.** Five posts, 28 accounts reached all day, every post tagged. Friday's five reached 76. The evidence over two weeks is that reach comes in ones (a reshare), not from the slot, so the right move is not to shuffle Saturday but to keep giving every post its tag and its note; no scheduling change this week.

**7. Facebook.** 23 referrals and a 40-view story page from a share the Ear did not make, on a network the Ear is not on. Logged, not acted on: a Facebook channel is a build, not a tweak, and the Instagram numbers are the job this task has.

**8. Zero saves, still.** 149 posts. Shares (16) and post-attributed follows (10) are the engagement that exists.

## 3. What changed

### The Reel is cut to 45 seconds *(shipped this run, first cut Wed Oct 21, move `shorter-reel`)*
Finding 3. `scripts/ig-reel.py`: `REEL_MAX_S = 45` is the default cap; a capped cut takes stories in priority order (`reel_priority`: the feature, then round-robin across Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun in episode order, then anything undated) and plays what it keeps in day order (`reel_order`). When the cap drops stories the intro is re-said as "Five of the eighteen things in South Knoxville this weekend. All of them at soknoear.com." (one new voice line; every quip is cached by text in `data/reel-tts/`). `--plan` prints the priority order with each story's day. `scripts/ig-reel.mjs`: `reelMaxSeconds(moves)` is 45 unless Andy answers "Don't" on `shorter-reel`, then 120; `publish-episode.mjs` reads the desk's moves (`deskMoves()`) and passes `--max`. The No. 18 cut at 45 s (5 stories, 45.2 s, 8.5 MB) is the dashboard's sample; the posted 116 s files are untouched. Test: "is cut to 45 seconds unless Andy said Don't" in `tests/ig-reel.test.ts`.

*Measures, Oct 26:* the Reel's reach against this week's 19 and against the week's stills; average watch above 20 s and skip rate under 50%; whether it gets a follow.

### Every story names its act, or the draft is refused *(shipped this run, live for the No. 19 draft Tue Oct 20, move `tag-the-artist`)*
Finding 4. `lib/schema.ts`: `social.igAct` is `{ key }` (a key into `content/ig-handles.json`, which must also be in `igTags` after the venue) or `{ none: "why" }`. `scripts/research-lib.mjs` check 4, for episodes dated from `ACT_GATE_FROM` (2026-10-21): a story without `igAct` is an error; a `key` not in `igTags` is an error (the picture wouldn't tag them); a `key` not in the registry is an error. `research-check.mjs` passes the registry in. The Tuesday task's step 1h names the field and the gate. `content/ig-handles.json` gains `ahun` → `@ahungryhungarian` (the DJ behind the Disco Demon night, verified from his own post, which was the story's source). Four tests in `tests/research-check.test.ts`.

*Measures, Oct 26:* every No. 19 story has `igAct`; how many carry a key; whether a post with an act tagged in the image beats the tagged-not-shared band (19).
