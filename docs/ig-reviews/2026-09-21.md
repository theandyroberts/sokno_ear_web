## 2. Findings

**1. The pipeline had its first fully clean week.** 13/13 posted on schedule, nothing retried, nothing failed, nothing stale. The lead cards opened at 13:00, inside the 09:00–13:00 window (A9). The standing cap held out two repeats (A10). The token refreshed on its Monday cron. The fixes from last week are working, and no pipeline item needs Andy.

**2. Weekly reach follows how many posts go out; per-post reach has been flat for four weeks.** No. 11–14 per post at comparable age: 6.6, 7.2, 6.3, 6.5. Weekly totals: 86, 79, 38, 84. **Nothing the pipeline changed in September moved per-post reach.** It only moved how reliably posts go out. A13 is confirmed and closed.

**3. The Sunday morning clump cost the week, and the cause was the collision rule.** Yoga (11:30), painting (11:00), mushrooms (12:30) and bluegrass (14:00) each wanted event minus 3h, which put two at 08:00 and one at 09:00. `slotFor` resolved collisions by +1h, so they went out 08/09/10/11: four Ijams banners in a row, reading 4, 5, 2, 5. The mushroom card, third in a row, read **2**, the lowest reach of any post on record. The 08:00 slot is also the second-worst hour on record (7.7). **Fixed** (A15): same-day posts now keep 2h apart. Collisions move earlier, which only adds lead time. Overflow goes to the day before. Replayed against No. 14, Sunday gets 2 posts instead of 4, and the other two go out Saturday at 14:00 and 21:00.

**4. First A/B week: image tags ahead, 7.8 vs 5.2.** It holds up without the Sunday clump (8.7 vs 6.7), and non-follower 28-day reach rose 6 → 9 in the same week. But the measure as written, per-post non-follower reach, **does not exist.** The Instagram API rejects `follow_type` on media insights. Two weeks remain. The readable measures are per-post reach and views by arm, plus the account-level non-follower trend.

**5. Ijams rose to 35% of the feed, and 8 of 13 posts this week.** That's an editorial fact: Ijams publishes the most events, and the episode covered them. The venue returns the least reach and has never engaged. Two things now cut its share without an editorial decision: the standing cap (two Ijams repeats held this week) and A15's spacing. A11 is the item that addresses the relationship itself.

**6. Followers passed 100, but growth slowed and follower reach keeps sliding.** +5 this week, 0.83/day, against 1.1–1.7 before. The feed reached 24% of followers in 28 days, down from 31% and 40%. Post-attributed follows are still zero across 92 posts.

**7. A second share, and profile visits moved for the first time in two weeks.** The Trailhead trivia card (Sep 3) got a share, and its story page is the second-most-read on the site. Profile visits from posts rose 21 → 26. Both are small. They're also the first engagement signals since August that aren't likes.

**8. Still zero comments and zero saves after 92 posts.** Thirteen weeks.
