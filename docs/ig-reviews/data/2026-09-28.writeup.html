07

Analysis

8 findings
1

The pipeline had its second clean week.

All 14 posts went out on schedule. Nothing retried, failed or went stale. The lead cards opened at 11:45 on a publish at about 11:30. The standing cap held out three repeats, and the token refreshed on its Monday cron. Last week’s desk shows no failed or dropped post.

2

A venue’s third and fourth banner of the day reads about half of its first two, and spacing them by the clock doesn’t help.

Thursday sent three Ijams banners at 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00. Saturday sent four at 09:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 19:00. They read 2, 2, 7 and 2, 6, 5, 1, an average of 3.6 against 6.3 for the week’s other seven posts. On record, a banner on a day carrying three or more from one venue averages 4.4, and on a day carrying one or two, 9.1. Fixed this run: a venue now gets two posts a day.

3

More posts stopped producing more reach.

Run 08 called it confirmed: weekly reach follows the number of posts. No. 15 sent the most posts of any week and will mature around 72–75, below No. 12’s 89 on eleven. The rule held while the extra posts were different venues. It didn’t hold when they were the same one.

4

Ijams is 41% of the feed, and nine of this week’s twelve story banners.

Its share has risen in every week but one since No. 10, and average reach has fallen with it. The venue cap spreads those posts across the week. It doesn’t reduce them. Ijams has still never liked, commented on or reshared one of the 31.

5

Follower growth stopped.

+1 on the week, with five straight days of no new follower, while outbound follows rose to +11. Post-attributed follows are still zero across 106 posts. Every follower the account has gained came from following someone first, and this week that returned one follower for eleven follows.

6

The image tag leads for the second week, and non-follower reach has risen in both weeks it has been live.

Tagged 7.3 against 5.5 over two weeks, and 7.3 against 4.4 on Ijams posts alone. Non-follower reach per window read 1 and 0 in the two weeks before the tag and 7 and 4 in the two since. The samples are 9 against 13 and every tagged post is one venue.

7

Five venue notes were drafted and none was sent.

The venue-notes pipeline went live this week. It found a named contact at each venue, wrote the note and put it in Gmail on Wednesday. Andy deleted all five, and the first notes go out with this week’s batch. This is venue outreach with the preparation already done: each note needs reading and sending.

8

Still zero comments and zero saves after 106 posts.

Fourteen weeks. Instagram sent no visit to the site this week. The four hashtags still make no measurable difference: posts without them average 12.1, posts with them 9.2 (n=10 vs 96). The episode-drop card read 4, the lowest it has read, and is the weakest recurring format for the ninth week.
