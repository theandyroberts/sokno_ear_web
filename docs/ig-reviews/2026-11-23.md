## 2. Findings

**1. The week was bad for one reason, and it was not the drip.** Twenty posts, every one on its slot but the Reel, every story banner tagged in the image, ten of seventeen with an act tagged too, the roundup tagged slide by slide. Follower views were normal. Seven venues got their weekly note on Wednesday. And one post in seventeen was shared. The same pipeline, one week after its best week, read 505 instead of 1,763 because The 808 and Ijams, who carried last week with three shares between them, shared nothing this week with seven posts between them. The reach of this account is a reshare decision made by someone at a venue, and nothing the Ear posts changes that decision after the fact.

**2. What can change it before the fact: the ask.** A tag is passive. The venue gets "soknoear tagged you in a post" and the post sits in its tagged tab; most weeks most venues leave it there. The 808 shared two posts the day after its first weekly note and none the week after its second. **The collaborator invite is the next rung on the ladder:** a notification that asks the venue to put the post on its own grid, where the post is theirs as much as ours. It costs the venue one tap and nothing else; a venue that ignores it is where it was. Shipped this run on every story banner, the venue only, with a fallback that posts without the invite if Meta refuses the container (the docs name no error for a refused invite, so the fallback catches any non-transient refusal and records it). It rides Wednesday as a Claude move; Andy's Don't leaves tags only. *Measure next Monday:* how many venues accepted (the post shows on their profile), the share rate, `droppedCollaborators` in the queue.

**3. The timing test is decided for early, on weak numbers, because the decision date arrived.** 9 of 27 against 6 of 27. Early led three weeks of four. The medians are identical. This is not a result anyone should quote as proof; it is the better of two sides on the named measure, chosen on the day the rule said to choose. The real finding of the four weeks is the one above: the share, not the hour, is the reach, and the hour's job is only to give the share room. A day's room is more room.

**4. The Reel's clock was never fixed.** The Nov 9 fix touched `ig-queue.mjs` and its tests and missed `ig-reel.mjs`, which built its own slot string with `-04:00`. The publish check prints the slot and does not read the post; the Nov 16 review wrote "Wed 12:01 reel" in its table and called the clock fix held. It did not hold for the Reel. Fixed with a test; the lesson from Nov 9 stands and gets a line: grep for the offset, not the file you remember.

**5. Ijams at four posts a week is the account's floor.** Twenty posts since October, three shares, and the seventeen unshared have a median of 22; this week's four read 15, 14, 12, 10, the bottom of the week. The park has the most events and the least reshares. The two-a-day cap already spreads them; the question is whether four Ijams banners a week are worth four slots. Not changed this run: the Ear is an event listing and the park is the district's biggest venue; the invite (finding 2) is the better first try, since Ijams' comms person is the one most likely to accept a collaboration and least likely to reshare by hand.

**6. The act gate worked and acts don't share.** Ten of seventeen banners tagged an act, up from seven. lespecial (Connecticut), Deadtronica, Marcel Sletten, Summoner's Circle, Goos:3 and the Fly by Night DJs were tagged; none shared. Planet Doom, a local promoter, shared yeEDM and that post read 129. Over seven weeks act-tagged posts are shared 31% of the time against 25% for venue-only: a real but small edge, and the acts that share are the local promoters, not the touring bands. The gate stays; it costs nothing; it closes after next week.

**7. Followers +25, the thumb stopped, and the two are the same fact.** Outbound follows −1 for the week after +96 the week before, and the daily gain fell from 6–9 a day to 0–5. Lifetime 402 gained on 391 followed. The follower line is Andy's hand; the review has said so every week and it is still true.

**8. The Reel reached 34, the most yet, watched a quarter through.** The widest Reel and the shortest attention since the 43-second cuts. Noise at this size; it goes out every week at Andy's call.

**9. The site had a better week than Instagram did.** 84 visitors against 71, Google 11; the Sourdough Saturday story read 10. The readers come from Google and the newsletter; Instagram sent 2.

## 3. What changed

### Every dated post goes out a day early *(shipped this run, from Wed Nov 25, move `all-posts-early`)*
Finding 3. `assignTimingArms` in `scripts/ig-schedule.mjs` takes `mode`: `"all-early"` (the default: every dated post 24 hours before its slot, same hour, never before publish day), `"split"` (the test as it ran) and `"day-of"`. `timingMode(moves)` reads Andy's answers: Don't on `all-posts-early` keeps the split; Don't on `early-posts` (the closed test move) puts everything back day-of. `scripts/ig-queue.mjs` uses it; the warning line names the mode.
