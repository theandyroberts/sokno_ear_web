## 2. Findings

**1. Per-post reach is noise at this size, and it has now misled three consecutive reviews.**
The weekly average has gone 16.1 → 10.5 → 10.3 → 13.7 → **8.0**. Each review read the latest move as a trend and built a plan on it; each following week reversed it. The baseline and Aug 13 reviews argued reach was collapsing and recommended cutting volume — the No. 9 week then produced the best numbers on record. The Aug 18 review retracted that and declared the decline over — this week is the worst on record. Meanwhile the account-level series says the audience was **flat between the two weeks (50 vs 51 person-days)**. With 66 followers and single-digit per-post counts, a swing of ±5 reach on a post is a couple of people. Post-level reach is not measuring what these reviews have been using it to measure.

**2. Followers grew +7 during the worst per-post week, so acquisition is not tracking feed reach either.**
Growth ran +1.17/day against a lifetime +0.88/day, in a week where post reach halved. Post-attributed follows read zero for all 49 posts including both growth weeks — that field is inert on this token, not informative. Whatever is adding followers is not visible in the post metrics, in either direction.

**3. The site has no clickable path from Instagram, and Umami confirms nobody is taking the unclickable one.**
Every caption ends with `soknoear.com` as **plain text**. Instagram does not linkify caption text — a reader would have to memorize it and type it. Umami's first four days: 16 sessions, **zero** with an instagram.com referrer, including the days the drip posted. The A5 question ("where are the followers coming from?") now has a partial answer for the reverse direction: the feed is sending essentially no traffic to the site, and structurally it cannot. Caveat: Instagram's in-app browser strips referrers on some paths, and a bio-link tap can land as direct — so "zero" is a strong signal, not a proof. It is worth noting the whole site drew 16 sessions in four days across *all* channels.

**4. Engagement is still nil, and profile visits stopped entirely.**
One share and zero comments and zero saves in 49 posts. Ten new posts this week added **zero** profile visits — the lifetime counter sat at 17 across the whole week. This finding has now survived four reviews unchanged, and no tagged venue has ever engaged with a post.

**5. Both pipeline changes shipped and worked mechanically; one shipped inverted to what was recommended.**
`19d44d0` (burst guard) works — Tuesday's four posts went out at 12:15 / 12:30 / 12:45 / 13:15, real spacing, no repeat of the 23-second dump. `a08a7a9` pins the promo pair to **open** the drip, which is the opposite of A3's "push the promo cards to later slots"; the commit message gives the editorial reason (they announce the episode, so they must precede the stories it introduces). That is Andy's call and it stands. The data cost was small and the two formats landed 9 and 6.
A3's own test failed: the Thursday 09:00 slot drew **8** against a ≥17 target. But the whole week ran at 8.0, so the slot test is confounded and proves nothing either way.

**6. The drip opened Tuesday for a Thursday–Sunday episode.**
Four of ten posts landed midday Tuesday Aug 18 for an episode covering Aug 20–23. Tuesday is the weakest day on record (avg 8.8, n=4, all of them this week). More to the point, it puts the episode announcement two days ahead of the weekend it describes and spends the drip's opening on a day nobody is planning from.

**7. One post went out by hand, outside the pipeline.**
A captionless carousel posted Fri Aug 21 15:53 ET ([DcUIi9JHzBJ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcUIi9JHzBJ/)) — not in `2026-08-19.json`. It drew 8, in line with the week. No caption means no venue tag, no hashtags, and no `soknoear.com`. Flagging it as a fact, not a fault — if hand-posting is now part of the routine, the queue should know about it so the spacing guard can account for it.

## 3. Action plan

### A1 — Change the metric this review steers on *(no code; procedural)*
Stop leading with per-post reach and stop writing action items off its weekly swings. From the next review, the headline numbers are:
1. **Account daily reach** (`period=day`, summed over the episode window) — the audience-size number.
2. **Follower trend** (per-day rate vs. prior windows).
3. **Engagement events** (likes + comments + saves + shares) in absolute counts.
Per-post reach stays in the report as a table, for spotting a single post that badly over- or under-performs — not as the thing the pipeline is tuned against. Two of the last three action plans would not have been written under this rule, and both were wrong.

### A2 — Give Instagram a clickable route to the site *(needs Andy — bio is manual)*
Finding 3. Two parts:
- **Andy:** confirm the profile's website field points at `soknoear.com` (it can't be read from a logged-out fetch, so I can't verify it). If it's empty, that is the single highest-value fix available this week.
- **Pipeline:** change the caption builder in `ig-queue.mjs` so the closing line reads **"Full story — link in bio"** on story banners and **"Read it and hear the audio briefing — link in bio"** on the episode-drop card, instead of the unlinkable bare `soknoear.com`. Cheap, one string per template.
*Measures:* Umami sessions with an instagram.com referrer, and total sessions on drip days vs. non-drip days. Umami now makes this checkable for the first time.

### A3 — Don't open the drip before the episode's Wednesday *(pipeline, low risk)*
Finding 6. In `ig-queue.mjs`, floor the first scheduled slot at the episode's own Wednesday evening — the promo pair still opens the run (that ordering stays, per Finding 5), it just can't open it two days early. This keeps the whole drip inside the window the episode actually covers, per the standing "only this weekend matters" rule.
*Measures:* no posts before Wednesday; Tuesday drops out of the day table.

### A4 — Run one real experiment instead of re-reporting fake ones *(pipeline, needs a feasibility check)*
The hashtag and @mention tables have never had a control and should stop being presented as findings. The one untested lever with a plausible mechanism for a local account is a **location tag** — Instagram surfaces tagged posts on place pages, which is the only discovery path in this pipeline that reaches people who don't already follow. Before proposing code: **verify that `location_id` is accepted on media creation through the Instagram Login API** (`graph.instagram.com`) — it is documented for the Facebook-Login Graph API and I have not confirmed it here. If supported, tag half the story banners with the venue's place ID for three weeks and compare.
*Measures:* reach and non-follower reach on tagged vs. untagged banners.

### A5 — Venue outreach *(needs Andy — carried forward, third review)*
Unchanged and still the largest available gain. Nine venues have been tagged across 49 posts and not one has ever liked, commented on, or reshared an Ear post. A single reshare by Ijams or Kern's would put the Ear in front of more people than the entire pipeline has reached in two months. This is a conversation, not a setting.

### Standing note
Two months in: 66 followers, 571 lifetime post-reach, one share, zero comments, zero saves, ~50 person-days of reach per week that has not grown in a fortnight, and zero measurable traffic to the site. The pipeline is doing exactly what it was built to do, reliably and on schedule. The constraint is not the pipeline — it is that the feed has no route out of its own follower list. A2 and A5 are the only two items here that could change that.
