The note went out Wednesday Nov 11; Friday it shared SHOGUN and Big Something within two hours of each other and read 273 and 275. It did not share Ahee, posted Wednesday evening (21). Three shows in one week gave the venue three chances, and the weekly note gave it a reason. The lesson is for the research pass as much as the drip: when a venue has three things on, run all three.

**3. Big Something has 27,600 followers and the post didn't tag them.** The act gate (`igAct`) was written so the research cannot skip the act, and it did not skip it: it wrote a reason, "the venue has not posted the show and its schedule names no handle for the band". That reason is where the search stopped, not where it should have. `instagram.com/bigsomething` is the band, Musician/band, followed by `@808knox`. The post reached 275 on the venue's share; a tag the band's own account could reshare into 27,600 feeds was left on the table. SHOGUN and Ahee got the same reason. **The gate now says: no handle in the venue's post is not a reason; look the act up by name, and `none` has to say what was tried.** It is a Claude move (`act-lookup`) so Andy can turn it off.

The other thirteen have a median of 22. The park's own share is worth more than ten tagged posts; the weekly note has been going to them since Oct 1. Nothing to change; the note keeps going.
