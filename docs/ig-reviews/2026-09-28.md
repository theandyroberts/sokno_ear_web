## 2. Findings

**1. The pipeline had its second clean week.** 14/14 posted on schedule, nothing retried, nothing failed, nothing stale. The lead cards opened at 11:45 on an ~11:30 publish, inside the 09:00–13:00 window. The standing cap held out three repeats. Last week's desk shows no failed or dropped post.

**2. A venue's third and fourth banner of the day reads about half of its first two, and spacing them by the clock does not help.** No. 15's Thursday sent three Ijams banners at 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00 and Saturday sent four at 09:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 19:00. A15 kept them two hours apart, as designed. They read 2, 2, 7 and 2, 6, 5, 1, an average of 3.6 against 6.3 for the week's other seven posts. No. 14's Sunday was the same shape. Across every day on record, a banner on a day carrying three or more from one venue averages 4.4, and on a day carrying one or two, 9.1. Measured against each post's own week the gap is 1.3 to 1.8 reach, so it isn't only the general decline. **Fixed** (A16).

**3. Volume stopped buying reach.** Last week's A13 said weekly reach follows the number of posts. No. 15 sent the most posts of any week and will mature around 72–75, below No. 12's 89 on 11. Unique weekly reach was flat at 22 and views fell 20%. The same people were reached and they were shown fewer of the posts. A13 held while the extra posts were different venues; it doesn't hold when they're the same one.

**4. Ijams is 41% of the feed and nine of this week's twelve story banners.** The share has risen every week but one since No. 10, and average reach has fallen with it. A16 spreads those posts across the week. It doesn't reduce them: No. 15 had nine Ijams posts for eight places under the cap, so one would still have gone out over it.

**5. Follower growth stopped.** +1 on the week, with five straight days of no new follower, while outbound follows rose to +11. Post-attributed follows are still zero across 106 posts. Every follower the account has gained came from following someone first, and this week that stopped working too.

**6. The image tag leads for the second week, and non-follower reach has risen in both weeks it has been live.** Tagged 7.3 against 5.5 over two weeks, and 7.3 against 4.4 on Ijams posts alone. Per-window non-follower reach read 1 and 0 in the two weeks before the tag and 7 and 4 in the two weeks since. n is 9 against 13 and every tagged post is one venue. It points the same way three different ways, and it's still small.

**7. Five venue notes were drafted and none was sent.** The venue-notes pipeline went live this week and put first-contact drafts for Kern's, Earl's, Ijams, Legacy Parks and Puckers in Gmail on Wednesday. All five expired unsent when their events passed. This is A11 with the work already done: the contact is found, the note is written, and each one needs reading and sending.

**8. Still zero comments and zero saves after 106 posts.** Fourteen weeks. Instagram sent no visit to the site this week, the first zero since No. 10.
