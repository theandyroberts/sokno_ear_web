## 2. Findings

**1. The pipeline had its third clean week and the carousel path works on the real Instagram.** 8/8 posted at their slots (the roundup moved 09:01 → 09:31 by the 20-minute gap). Both carousels went as carousels: ten child containers and a parent for the weekend, three and a parent for Ijams, no `downgradedFrom`. Last week's desk shows no failed or dropped post. The token was refreshed this morning.

**2. Followers +10, the best week in four, on +20 follows and one unfollow.** Growth returned the week after it stopped, and it came the same way it always has — following first. The ratio was the worst on record (0.50) because the follows were the most on record. Profile views were the highest of any window (13). Post-attributed follows: zero, 114 posts.

**3. Fewer posts reached fewer people.** Weekly reach follows the number of posts from different venues; this week tested the other side of that. Eight posts instead of fourteen: per-post reach up 20% at equal age, total reach down 32% (47 v 69), unique reach down 24% (19 v 25), views down 52%, person-days 35 against 43–47. The roundup was built to lift the floor, and it did (lowest post 4, was 1). It was not built to cut the audience, and it did that too.

**4. Both carousels read 4.** The weekend roundup read what the episode card it replaced read — no better, no worse, 0 saves on "Save it for Saturday", posted between two other posts in a 45-minute window. The venue carousel is the clearer failure: three Ijams stories in one post read 4, where Ijams singles this week read 5–7 each and last week's caption-only Ijams singles read 5.5 on average. **Decision: every story keeps its single post again; the weekend roundup stays on top** as the one swipeable, saveable object a local account should have, and gets read again at No. 17 and No. 18 for saves and for whether it ever beats 6.

**5. Tag every venue post in the image.** Finding 5 above, and the table. Shipped.

**6. The first notes went out and venue outreach closes at 48 days.** Five venues are `introduced`; from here `scripts/venue-notify.mjs` sends their weekly note itself. Nothing has come back yet: Ijams has 34 posts and no like, comment or reshare. The measure for the coming weeks is the one that has mattered since the baseline — one reshare from Ijams, Kern's or Puckers.

**7. Posts earn reach for two weeks, not two days.** No. 15 grew 35% after age 2–5 and No. 14 grew 15% after age 8. Every "lowest on record" said at a Monday review about the week just gone has been 25–35% too low. Revised in the equal-age section; the dashboard's reach-per-post line is read at the same age each week so it stays comparable.

**8. First comment, zero saves, one Instagram visit to the site.** Fifteen weeks. The one format that reaches non-followers at scale — a Reel — is built as a sample and is on the dashboard.
