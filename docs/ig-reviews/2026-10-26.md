## 2. Findings

**1. The best week the account has had, and it is the same account.** 966 at age 1–5 beats No. 17's 926; the mean is 50.8 and the median is 21. Three weeks of post-by-post data say the same thing in the same proportion: 13 shared posts at 148, 32 tagged-not-shared at 22. This week's three big posts were three different venues — Ijams (never before), the Pink Cactus (second time), Old Sevier (first post, first share) — which is the healthiest version of the pattern: the reach isn't one venue's goodwill.

**2. Tagging the act works, and it was the gate that made it run.** The code for tagging the act was live Oct 12 and tagged nobody for a week because the draft never named one. Making `igAct` a required field with a check behind it produced 16 of 16 the first time it ran. Of the seven posts that tagged an act, three were shared (43%); of the ten that tagged the venue alone, two (20%). Small numbers, one week, but the direction is the one the Oct 12 reasoning predicted: a second account with its own crowd is a second chance at a reshare. The move is closed as done; the gate stays. Eight of 16 stories had no act to tag, and that is right — a paddle led by the park's naturalists has no handle to guess.

**3. The Reel's problem is its first eleven seconds, not its length.** Cutting 116 s to 46 s moved the completion numbers the way Instagram's distribution wants (skip 68% → 44%, watched 10% → 24%) and moved reach not at all (19 → 16). Average watch was 11.8 s then 11.1 s. The first eleven seconds of both cuts are the same thing: the episode card with "Six of the sixteen things to do in South Knoxville this weekend." A viewer scrolling past sees a title card and a counting line and leaves. The feature's artwork and quip is the strongest four seconds the cut has, and it was playing second. So it plays first; the title card becomes the bridge and still says soknoear.com in the middle (Andy's ask), the outro says it at the end. This is the Reel's last structural fix: if the third cut still reads under the week's median still post (21 this week), the Reel costs ElevenLabs credits and a slot for nothing and comes out on Nov 2.

**4. Ijams, 54 posts, one share, 346.** The park has had more Ear posts than anyone (54 of 168) at a median of 11, and the review has said for weeks that Ijams does not reshare. It did this once and the post is the best the account has had. The weekly note has gone to Ijams every Wednesday since Oct 7. There is no move in this; the point is that the venue-note channel is the only lever and it is already pulled every week.

**5. The follows.** +85 outbound follows in a week is a new thing; the previous high was 34. It bought part of +49. The ratio has been climbing for a month (0.72 → 0.89 → 1.10) and dropped to 0.90 this week. Not a pipeline matter — nothing in the repo follows anyone — and not a problem at this size, but the follower tile should be read with it in mind: around 25–30 of this week's followers would have come anyway on the pattern of the last two weeks (+45, +39), the rest are follow-backs.

**6. The roundup read 37 and got the first save.** 14 → 32 → 79 (No. 18, now) → 37. One save, the first in 168 posts. Still the post every venue in the week is told about. Stays.

**7. The timing test is the first scheduling change aimed at the right variable.** Every slot and day analysis since August has said the same thing: medians 9–17 everywhere. Scheduling has never moved reach because reach doesn't come from the slot; it comes from a reshare. Posting a day early gives the venue a day to do that before doors. It starts Wednesday; nothing to read yet.

**8. Zero comments in three weeks, one save ever.** Shares (21), profile visits (109) and attributed follows (16) are the engagement that exists.
