Decorative horizontal rule with a center ornament — separates sections and keeps the old-print rhythm. Reach for it between stacked stories on the one-page scroll.

```jsx
<Divider ornament="star" />
<Divider ornament="diamond" color="rust" />
<Divider ornament="double" thickness={1} />
```

Ornaments: `star` (default ★), `diamond` (◆), `flourish` (❧), `triple` (✦ ✦ ✦), or `double` (two parallel rules, no ornament). Colors `ink` / `rust` / `teal`.
