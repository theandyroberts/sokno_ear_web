The recurring neighborhood radio dispatch — a big round play button, a hand-set waveform, a time readout, and a short description. Styled like the paper, never a glossy podcast widget.

```jsx
<AudioBriefing
  title="Weekend Audio Briefing"
  description="Your 90-second look at what's happening around SoKno."
  duration="01:30"
/>
```

The play button toggles a play/pause state and fills part of the waveform rust. Drop it near the feature story at the top of the weekly page.
